## 1. Definitions

In this Agreement:

- **"Adventure"** means a treasure-hunt experience configured by an Arranger, including its puzzles, media, accept-lists, and location data.
- **"Affiliate"** means an entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.
- **"Arranger"** means an authorised user of the Service who designs or operates an Adventure on behalf of Customer.
- **"Customer Data"** means all data submitted to the Service by or on behalf of Customer, including Arranger account data, Adventure content, Player Data, and operational metadata generated during use.
- **"Documentation"** means the user-facing operational and technical documentation for the Service published by Zonventure, including the README, OPERATIONS, SELFHOSTING, and in-product help, as updated from time to time.
- **"Hosted Service"** means the Service operated by Zonventure on infrastructure controlled by Zonventure and made available to Customer over the public internet.
- **"Order"** or **"Order Form"** means a written or electronic ordering document signed or accepted by both Parties that references this Agreement and sets out the commercial terms of a specific subscription, license, or engagement.
- **"Player"** means an individual end-user who participates in an Adventure (a member of a team taking part in an event).
- **"Player Data"** means personal data of Players collected through the Service, as further described in the DPA.
- **"Self-Hosted Service"** means the Service operated by Customer (or its designated provider) on infrastructure controlled by Customer.
- **"Service"** means the Zonventure software platform, including the Hosted Service and the Self-Hosted Service, and any related materials provided by Zonventure under an Order.
- **"Subscription Term"** means the period during which Customer is entitled to use the Service under an Order.

## 2. Service

### 2.1 Provision

Subject to Customer's payment of the applicable fees and compliance with this Agreement, Zonventure will make the Service available to Customer in accordance with the relevant Order and the Documentation.

### 2.2 License grants

- **Hosted Service.** Zonventure grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, worldwide, limited right during the Subscription Term to access and use the Hosted Service for Customer's internal business purposes.
- **Self-Hosted Service.** Zonventure grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, worldwide license during the Subscription Term to install, run, and use the Self-Hosted Service on Customer-controlled infrastructure for Customer's internal business purposes. This license includes the right to make a reasonable number of internal copies for backup, testing, and disaster-recovery purposes.

### 2.3 Restrictions

Customer will not, and will not permit any third party to:

(a) sell, rent, lease, sublicense, or commercially exploit the Service except as expressly permitted by an Order;
(b) reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble any part of the Service except to the extent expressly permitted by applicable law;
(c) use the Service to build a competing product or service;
(d) remove or alter any proprietary notices in the Service or Documentation;
(e) use the Service to store or process unlawful, infringing, harassing, discriminatory, or sexually exploitative content;
(f) probe, scan, or stress-test the Hosted Service without prior written permission from Zonventure;
(g) circumvent any usage limits set out in an Order, the Documentation, or the Service itself.

### 2.4 Updates

Zonventure may modify the Service from time to time, including by adding, changing, or removing features. Zonventure will not materially diminish the core functionality of a paid feature within an active Subscription Term without giving Customer reasonable notice and a refund of pre-paid fees attributable to the diminished period if Customer chooses to terminate the affected Order in response.

### 2.5 Beta features

Features designated by Zonventure as "beta", "preview", "experimental", or similar are provided **as is** and without warranty of any kind. Zonventure may discontinue beta features at any time.

## 3. Customer obligations

### 3.1 Acceptable use

Customer is responsible for the use of the Service by its Arrangers and Players. Customer will, and will ensure each of its users will:

(a) comply with all applicable laws in connection with their use of the Service;
(b) use the Service only for lawful purposes and in accordance with this Agreement, the Order, and the Documentation;
(c) obtain all consents and rights required to upload, share, and use Customer Data with the Service, including from Players (and from the parents or guardians of any minors), particularly with respect to location data;
(d) take reasonable steps to verify that physical locations used in Adventures are safe, lawful to access, and appropriate for the intended audience;
(e) notify Zonventure promptly of any suspected unauthorised use of the Service or compromise of Customer's credentials.

### 3.2 Arranger content

Customer is solely responsible for Adventure content it creates, imports, or modifies, including any text, media, accept-list answers, location coordinates, and notes. Zonventure does not pre-screen Customer-authored content.

### 3.3 Player consent

Customer will inform Players of how their data will be used during the Adventure (including location processing) before they begin participating. Where required by applicable law, Customer will obtain affirmative consent before collecting Player Data.

## 4. Fees and payment

### 4.1 Fees

Customer will pay the fees set out in each Order in the currency stated. Unless otherwise stated, fees are exclusive of value-added tax and any similar transaction-based taxes, which Customer is responsible for paying.

### 4.2 Invoicing and payment terms

Zonventure will invoice Customer in accordance with the schedule set out in the Order. Unless otherwise specified, payment is due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law.

### 4.3 Suspension for non-payment

Zonventure may suspend Customer's access to the Hosted Service if an undisputed invoice is more than thirty (30) days past due, after giving Customer at least ten (10) business days' written notice and an opportunity to cure.

### 4.4 Fee changes

Zonventure may change its fees at the start of each Renewal Term by giving Customer at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice. Customer's sole remedy in the event of an unacceptable fee change is to elect not to renew.

### 4.5 Taxes

Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income. For B2B sales within the EU, the Parties intend the reverse-charge mechanism to apply where available; Customer will provide a valid VAT identification number on request.

## 5. Intellectual property

### 5.1 Service IP

As between the Parties, Zonventure retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Service, the Documentation, and any improvements, modifications, derivative works, and feedback received from Customer. No rights are granted to Customer except those expressly stated in this Agreement.

### 5.2 Customer Data

As between the Parties, Customer retains all right, title, and interest in and to Customer Data. Customer grants Zonventure a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to host, copy, transmit, and display Customer Data solely as necessary to provide the Service to Customer and to comply with applicable law.

### 5.3 Aggregated data

Zonventure may collect, use, and publish aggregated, anonymised data derived from Customer's use of the Service for purposes of operating, improving, and benchmarking the Service, provided that such data does not identify Customer, any Arranger, or any Player.

### 5.4 Feedback

Customer may, but is not required to, provide feedback, suggestions, and recommendations to Zonventure. Customer grants Zonventure a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use such feedback without restriction.

## 6. Confidentiality

### 6.1 Definition

**"Confidential Information"** means non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other (the "Receiving Party") that is identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes generally known through no fault of the Receiving Party, (b) was known to the Receiving Party before receipt without confidentiality obligation, (c) is rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligation, or (d) is independently developed without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

### 6.2 Obligations

The Receiving Party will (a) use Confidential Information only as necessary to perform under this Agreement, (b) protect Confidential Information using at least the same care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar sensitivity (and in any event reasonable care), and (c) not disclose Confidential Information except to its employees, contractors, and advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Section.

### 6.3 Compelled disclosure

If the Receiving Party is compelled by law to disclose Confidential Information, it will (where lawful and practicable) notify the Disclosing Party in advance and cooperate in seeking a protective order or other appropriate remedy at the Disclosing Party's expense.

## 7. Data protection

The processing of personal data by Zonventure on Customer's behalf is governed by the Data Processing Agreement at `legal/DPA.md`, which is incorporated into this Agreement by reference and applies whenever Zonventure processes Customer's personal data as a processor (including in the Hosted Service). For the Self-Hosted Service, Customer is the controller and processor of its own data, and the DPA does not apply.

## 10. Indemnification

### 10.1 By Zonventure

Zonventure will defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Service, when used by Customer in accordance with this Agreement, infringes a third party's intellectual property right, and will pay any amounts finally awarded against Customer (or agreed in settlement) attributable to such claim, provided that Customer (a) gives Zonventure prompt written notice, (b) gives Zonventure sole control of the defence and settlement, and (c) provides reasonable cooperation at Zonventure's expense.

If the Service becomes, or in Zonventure's reasonable opinion is likely to become, the subject of an infringement claim, Zonventure may, at its option and expense: (i) obtain the right for Customer to continue using the Service, (ii) modify the Service so it is non-infringing, or (iii) terminate the affected Order and refund pre-paid fees for the unused portion of the Subscription Term. **This Section states Zonventure's entire liability and Customer's sole remedy for IP infringement claims.**

This indemnity does not apply to claims arising from (1) Customer Data, (2) Customer's modifications to the Service, (3) use of the Service in combination with software or hardware not provided or recommended by Zonventure, where the infringement would not have occurred but for the combination, or (4) use of a beta feature.

### 10.2 By Customer

Customer will defend Zonventure against any third-party claim arising out of (a) Customer Data, (b) an Adventure operated by or on behalf of Customer (including any incident involving a Player), (c) Customer's breach of Section 3 (Customer obligations) or 5.2 (Customer Data), or (d) Customer's violation of applicable law in its use of the Service, and will pay any amounts finally awarded against Zonventure (or agreed in settlement) attributable to such claim.

### 11.1 Cap

EXCEPT FOR THE EXCLUDED CLAIMS BELOW, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER TO ZONVENTURE UNDER THE APPLICABLE ORDER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE LIABILITY.

### 11.2 Excluded damages

EXCEPT FOR THE EXCLUDED CLAIMS BELOW, NEITHER PARTY WILL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS, REVENUE, GOODWILL, OR DATA, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

### 11.3 Excluded claims

The limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to (a) breach of confidentiality (Section 6), (b) Customer's payment obligations, (c) either Party's indemnification obligations under Section 10, (d) Customer's breach of Section 2.3 (Restrictions) or Section 5.1 (Service IP), or (e) liabilities that cannot be limited under applicable law.

## 12. Term and termination

### 12.1 Term

This Agreement is effective on the Effective Date and continues until all Orders have expired or been terminated.

### 12.2 Termination for cause

Either Party may terminate this Agreement (or an affected Order) on written notice if the other Party (a) materially breaches the Agreement and fails to cure within thirty (30) days after written notice, or (b) becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to bankruptcy proceedings that are not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

### 12.3 Effect of termination

On termination or expiration: (a) Customer's right to use the Service ends; (b) Customer will pay Zonventure all amounts due as of the effective date of termination; (c) Zonventure will, on Customer's written request received within thirty (30) days, make Customer Data from the Hosted Service available for export in a structured format and will then delete Customer Data in accordance with the DPA; (d) each Party will return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information.

### 12.4 Survival

Sections that by their nature should survive termination will survive, including Sections 1, 2.3, 4 (for accrued obligations), 5, 6, 9.3, 10, 11, 12.3, 12.4, and 13.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Sweden, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Stockholm, Sweden, except that either Party may seek injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

### 13.3 Notices

Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and will be deemed given when delivered by recognised courier or by email to the addresses identified in the Order, with a copy to legal@[ZONVENTURE DOMAIN] for notices to Zonventure.

### 13.8 Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions will remain in full force, and the unenforceable provision will be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable while preserving the Parties' intent.
