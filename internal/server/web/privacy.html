What we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the adventure platform:

  • Account data for arrangers: email, organization name, hashed password, IP and user-agent at login. Used for authentication and security audit.
  • Player data: a slot name (chosen by the arranger), and during an event the player's GPS location and answer text. Used to display progress on the live board and verify location gates.
  • Requests for a quote: the name, company, email, phone and answers someone sends an organisation through its request form. The organisation asked is responsible for them; we deliver them to it and keep them for it.
  • Operational logs: login successes/failures, IPs, access timestamps. Used to detect abuse and investigate issues.
  • Email-verification and password-reset tokens, one-shot, expire in 24 hours (email verification) or 1 hour (password reset), deleted on use.

What we DON'T collect

  • No analytics or advertising trackers.
  • No payment-card numbers — payment is processed by Stripe; we receive only their non-sensitive event metadata.
  • No location data from arrangers, only from players who opt into geolocation when prompted by the play page.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete the account. Deletion has a 7-day grace window during which you can cancel by logging back in; after that the data is permanently removed.
  • Player location pings & answers: {{LOCATION_RETENTION}}
  • Party adventures booked for children: the whole party adventure — teams, player names, answers and photos — {{PARTY_RETENTION}} The payment record is kept for bookkeeping, without the booker's email.
  • Party adventures made without an account: an unpaid draft is deleted 30 days after it was last changed. A paid one — its cards, teams, player names and answers, and the email it was saved to — is deleted when its 14 days of play are over, or when it lapses unplayed, unless its buyer keeps it in an account of their own. The payment record is kept for bookkeeping, without the email. Email links expire after an hour; a sign-in on a browser after 30 days.
  • Postal addresses for letters we post to you: the name and address {{ADDRESS_RETENTION}} The order is kept for bookkeeping.
  • Requests for a quote: deleted a year after their last change, or sooner when the organisation deletes one.
  • Audit logs: {{AUDIT_RETENTION}}
  • Verification / reset tokens: 24 hours (verification) or 1 hour (reset), or until used.

Your rights

  • Access — you can download your data at any time from Settings → "Download my data" (a JSON file).
  • Erasure — Settings → "Delete account" wipes your account and (if you're the last member) your organization. Players can wipe their per-event data via the "Forget me" menu inside /play.
  • Rectification — change your email or password from Settings.
  • Portability — the export is a structured, machine-readable JSON document.
  • Objection / restriction — contact us at {{DPO_EMAIL}}.

Cookies we use

We use only first-party cookies that are strictly necessary for the service to function. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers. Specifically:

  • zv_session & zv_slot — your login session for the arranger and player surfaces. HttpOnly so client JavaScript can't read them, expires when the session ends.
  • zv_csrf — anti-CSRF token used to confirm that mutating requests come from this site. Not HttpOnly because the page's own JS must read it to echo it back.

Because all three cookies are strictly necessary for a service you explicitly requested (article 5(3) ePrivacy Directive exemption), we don't display a consent banner. We will if and when we ever introduce non-essential cookies; this section will be updated at the same time.

We also use the browser's localStorage for UI preferences (theme, language, "don't show this banner again" dismissals). No personal data, no third parties.

Children

Most players are adults in arranged team activities (corporate team-builds, public city walks, etc.) and the platform's defaults assume that audience.

Players under 13 are supported via an explicit COPPA path the arranger acknowledges before scheduling: the arranger attests that the activity is run by their school / camp / parent organisation and that they have the parental consent required by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act for any U.S.-based minor participants. Independently of that flag, arrangers are responsible for ensuring minors don't participate without appropriate parental consent in their jurisdiction (see Terms).
